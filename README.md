# **IBM HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Project Overview**

This project focuses on conducting **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on the IBM HR Employee Attrition dataset. The primary goal is to uncover hidden patterns behind employee turnover, compensation structures, job satisfaction levels, and work-life balance dynamics across different corporate departments.

---

# **Key Insights & Analytical Focus**
* **Compensation Scaling:** Monthly income scales primarily with **Job Level** rather than department boundaries, rising sharply from entry-level positions to executive tiers.
* **Satisfaction & Retention:** Employee satisfaction tiers (1 through 4) highlight critical retention pressure points, especially within support and HR functions where dissatisfaction rates peak.
* **Work-Life Balance:** Over **60%** of the workforce consistently rates their work-life balance within the upper tier ("Better"), establishing a baseline benchmark for organizational well-being.

---

# **Technologies Used & Why They Were Chosen**

* **Python:** Chosen as the primary programming language for its flexibility, readability, and extensive ecosystem for data manipulation.
* **Pandas:** Utilized for data cleaning, aggregation, grouping (`groupby`), pivoting, and calculating outlier-resistant metrics such as medians and percentage distributions.
* **Matplotlib:** Used for generating clean, straightforward visualizations (bar charts, line plots, and pie charts) without relying on overly complex functional wrappers or heavy abstraction layers.
